SECTION 06The measure

A printed scale, not a gauge. The measure states a position on a bounded scale, names the band that position falls in, and shows the evidence that permits the claim. When there is no estimate, it shows no position at all.

6.1 Why a rule, not a dial

A dial implies a target and a safe zone. A needle implies a live reading of a physical quantity. A ring implies completeness: at 100% the ring closes and the task is done. None of those metaphors belong to a text estimate that is bounded, fallible, and never a verdict.

The banded rule comes from printed reports: a bounded axis, divided into named intervals, with a mark at the measured value. It is precise without being mechanical. It can be read at a glance, read again with a screen reader, and understood without colour.

One sentence

The measure never fills. A fill implies progress toward a goal. Trust is not a goal.

The six stops are always present, always solid, and always in the same order. Only the marker moves.

Refused

No speedometer arc, no needle, no percentage ring, no traffic light, no star rating, no single-colour progress bar, no animated sweep from zero. Each of them adds certainty the model does not have.

The widths are the score intervals themselves: 20 points for the two outer manipulation bands, 15 for the middle bands, 15 for the top organic band. Equalising them would be tidier and false. A reader who learns the track should be able to see that band 1 is twice as wide as band 3.

Copy rule

The six band names, their boundaries, and their order are fixed in product-decisions.md. The measure may never abbreviate a band name, merge two bands, or add a seventh “neutral” zone.

6.6 Motion and behaviour

  • The marker appears at its value. It never sweeps in from zero, and the score never counts up. Counting up stages certainty as an event.
  • Rescan crossfades. 120 ms opacity on the measure block; no slide, no rotation, no width animation.
  • No fill animation. The stops are static. Only the marker's left position changes, and only between two scored values.
  • Unknown replaces, not empties. The scored measure is removed and the unknown block takes its place. It does not linger at 40% opacity.
  • No number formatting. No commas, no decimals, no “out of 100”. The scale labels already say 100.
  • The accessible name carries the whole measure. Score, band, evidence, and direction of the marker are announced together; the six stops are not read as six separate items.

Rules

  • Sentence case, verb first. “Retry”, not “Retry analysis” and never “SUBMIT”. Three words maximum. Action labels come from the copy deck.
  • 36 px high, 30 px small. 2 px radius. No pill, no shadow, no gradient, no icon-only buttons except settings and close, both with an accessible name.
  • Hover darkens. Petrol to petrol-deep. No lift, no scale, no glow.
  • One primary per view. The panel usually has none; the error state owns it.
  • Disabled needs a reason. If the user cannot act, the hint next to the control says why. Prefer hiding an impossible action over greying it out.

Focus ring

2 px petrol, 2 px offset, square corners. On review rows the ring is drawn inside the row (inset 0 0 0 2px) so the list does not jump. The ring is never removed for mouse users and never replaced by a colour change alone.

Hit areas

44 × 44 px minimum for every interactive element. A 26 px chip gets an invisible 44 px target. Text links inside body copy are exempt from the 44 px rule but still need 3:1 contrast and a visible focus ring.

Rules

  • Five maximum. Order by absolute contribution, then by the fixed taxonomy order. No scroll inside the list.
  • Fixed labels. The seven taxonomy names are used verbatim. Helper summaries are reviewed strings, never generated at runtime.
  • Never a quote. No review sentence, fragment, reviewer name, avatar, or star rating appears in a signal row.
  • Both directions exist. A review can carry manipulation and organic signals at once. That is what “Mixed signals” means.
  • Contribution is optional. Without a stable, validated value, show the label and direction only; no percentage, no bar.
  • Never renormalise the visible subset. Contributions are normalized across all features; the five returned signals do not add to 100%. Never rescale them to look like a whole.

Accessible name

“Reads like promotional copy, toward manipulation, 31 percent contribution.” The arrow is hidden from assistive technology; the words carry the direction. The axis is decorative and not announced.

No moral colour

Rust and petrol are directions, not good and bad. Never add a green check to an organic signal or a red cross to a manipulation signal. The band name already carries the judgment, and it is a judgment about text patterns.

What the evidence allows

This review has 20–49 words of analysable text. The estimate is shown as indicative: the direction may be useful, the exact position is not.

At least 50 words are needed for adequate evidence. Under 20 words, TrustLoop shows no estimate at all.

Evidence describes the text, not the reviewer.

Host name, local time, result. No review text, no URL, no signal labels.

Stored

Time, host name, adapter ID, status, score and verdict when scored, evidence quality, engine, model version, and reason code when unknown. Local only; never synchronised.

Never stored

Review text, review keys, reviewer names, page titles, URL paths, persistent text fingerprints, or the number of reviews. History is a list of where and when, not what was said.

Off by default

History is opt-in. The switch starts off. Turning it off stops new entries; existing entries remain until cleared.

Bounded

Capped at 100 entries or 30 days, whichever comes first. “Clear history” removes every local entry after a confirmation that names what will be lost.

The interface shows host, time, and the result; the record may also carry the adapter, engine, model version, and reason code for support. The empty state is “No scans yet.” followed by “Open a supported product page to start.” No illustration, no mascot, no suggestion to browse.
